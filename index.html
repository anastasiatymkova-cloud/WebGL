About the Model

This application renders a pear-shaped mathematical surface defined by a parametric equation using indexed triangles and WebGL. The model extends Practical Assignment 2 by introducing three texture maps: diffuse, specular and normal. The diffuse texture controls the surface color, while the specular texture controls the intensity of reflected highlights. The normal texture provides per-pixel normals for detailed surface lighting. Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization is used to construct an orthonormal TBN basis. According to Variant 18, the normal vector is prioritized and the tangent is corrected. The application also includes a rotating light source, interactive model rotation and adjustable surface granularity.
